# Privacy Policy — PDF Reader, Editor & Tools

**Developer:** Daily Applications. **Contact:** dailyapplications@gmail.com.

## The short version
- The app has **no account, no server and no cloud upload**. Your PDFs, images and the files the app creates stay on your device.
- The developer does **not** collect or receive your documents or personal information.
- The app is free and shows **ads from Google AdMob**. Google and its partners may process some data to show ads, as described below, and only according to your choices.

## What stays on your device
Files you open or create are processed locally. The app stores a small amount of data on your device to work: the list of recently opened files (names and links), bookmarks and last-read page, your credit balance and settings (such as reading mode and whether to ask where to save files), and counters used to time ads. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Files you save remain where you saved them until you delete them.

## Permissions
The app asks for no storage, camera or contacts permissions. It uses the system file picker, the system "save as" screen and the Android sharing and printing features. The document scanner (if you use Scan) is provided by Google Play services. The app uses the internet connection only to load ads and to show Google's consent form.

## Ads and consent (Google AdMob)
The app displays ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on your region and choices, Google and its ad partners may use identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address (and a coarse location derived from it) and device information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and some US states) the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under **Settings → Privacy options**. If you decline, you can still use the app; ad-related features such as earning credits by watching an ad may then be unavailable, and the daily free credits continue. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Credits
Credits are a local counter on your device used to limit some PDF tools. They have no monetary value, cannot be transferred and are not tied to an account. Clearing app data or reinstalling resets them.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by your country). Please do not use it if you are below that age without a parent's permission.

## Your rights
Because the developer does not hold your personal data, there is normally nothing to access or delete. For data processed by Google, use Google's own tools (for example Ads Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com) and the Privacy options inside the app. If you have questions, contact dailyapplications@gmail.com.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the date above updated.
